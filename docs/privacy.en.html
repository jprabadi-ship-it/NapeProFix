Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

NapeProFix ("the app") customizes the trackball gestures of the Keychron Nape Pro on macOS. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information collected

The app collects and transmits no personal information. Specifically, it does none of the following:

  • Collect identifying information (name, email address, account details)
  • Analyze usage (analytics, telemetry, automatic crash reporting)
  • Display advertising, or use data for advertising purposes
  • Provide or sell data to third parties

The app makes no network connections. Every feature behaves identically with no internet connection.

Information stored on your Mac

The app stores the following only on your own Mac. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

ContentsLocation
Assignments for the four gesture directions, per-layer settings, the layer-switch shortcut, the rotation correction angle, and scrolling settings ~/Library/Preferences/ (the standard macOS preferences area)

The app does not create a log file.

Handling of input

The app requires macOS Accessibility access in order to observe key events.

This is needed to detect the four keys the trackball gestures send (f13, pad/, padclear, help) along with any layer-switch key you register, and to replace them with the action you assigned. Observed input is never stored or transmitted.

Keys pressed while assigning a keyboard shortcut, or while recording a layer-switch key, are saved as part of that assignment. That is configuration you entered deliberately, not a record of your input.

Permission used

Cost and external sites

The app is distributed free of charge and contains no payments of any kind. The developer receives no payment information.

The app and this site link to GitHub and to an Amazon wishlist. If you follow those links, the handling of information there is governed by each service's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing to those services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the last-updated date above will be revised and the change described.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, contact admin@gigowat.com.
